RECOMMENDED INFERENCE SETTINGS
==============================

Confidence Threshold: 0.25 (adjustable 0.1-0.9)
IOU Threshold: 0.45
Image Size: 640 (default) or 1280 (higher accuracy, slower)

For live detection:
- Lower confidence (0.20-0.30) for more detections
- Higher confidence (0.50-0.70) for fewer false positives
- Adjust IOU (0.40-0.60) to control overlapping detections

KNOWN CONSIDERATIONS
====================

1. Training showed systematic instability at epochs 5 and 29
   - Epoch 27 checkpoint selected as most stable
   - Future training may benefit from adjusted learning rate schedule

2. Model trained on synthetic data
   - Performance on real playmat photos may vary
   - Expect excellent performance on similar synthetic images
   - Real-world testing recommended for your specific use case

3. Large class count (2,641)
   - Some rare cards may have limited training examples
   - Common cards expected to have higher accuracy
   - Consider class-specific confidence thresholds if needed
